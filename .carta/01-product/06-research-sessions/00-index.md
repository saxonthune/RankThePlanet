# Research Sessions

A session lands here when its findings are useful past the original task — recurring techniques, library quirks, surprising root causes. The goal is to shorten the next investigation that touches the same surface area.

Each session doc describes a problem domain in intent voice: what the system does, how to instrument it, what the failure mode looks like, and the solution. Historical narrative (what was tried first, who debugged it, when) belongs in commit messages and PR descriptions, not here.
